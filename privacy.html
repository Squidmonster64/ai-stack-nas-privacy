Privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026.

Who uses it. AI Stack NAS (DHJ) is used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google and Dropbox accounts. It is not a public service.

What it accesses. With the owner's consent: Gmail messages and attachments (read), Gmail drafts, and sending mail only to the owner's own address (gmail.readonly, gmail.compose); Google Calendar events (read-only, calendar.readonly); Dropbox files (read, and creating new files in one inbox folder).

Where data goes. Data is processed on the owner's private network-attached storage device. OAuth tokens are stored there in owner-only files. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train generalised AI models. Content may be passed to an AI model provider only to answer the owner's own requests.

Google API data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in Dropbox's connected-apps settings; the owner deletes stored tokens on revocation.

Contact. Use the developer contact shown on the Google consent screen.
